About the Label

Blue Hyena Records LLC is a forward‑thinking independent label built for creators who break molds, bend genres, and build movements. We champion artists with vision — musicians who refuse to be boxed in or defined by industry expectations.

Our mission is simple: empower independent artists with the tools, platforms, and creative freedom they need to thrive.
