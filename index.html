About the label

Who we are

Blue Hyena Records LLC

Blue Hyena Records is an independent label focused on unique voices, experimental sounds, and artists who live just left of center. We support creative freedom, honest storytelling, and music that feels like it was made at 3 a.m. with the lights low.

Independent • Artist-first • Genre-fluid
What we do

Releases, radio, and more

From digital releases to curated radio shows, Blue Hyena Records is building a space where artists can be heard without compromise. We use Blue Hyena Radio as a living, breathing showcase of the label’s sound and community.
